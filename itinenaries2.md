15 DAY SAFARI AND ZANZIBAR 


The Safari (Days 1–8)

Day 1: Arrival. Land at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) and transfer to a hotel in Arusha.

Day 2: Tarangire National Park. Drive to Tarangire (about 2 hours). Famed for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees.

Day 3: Lake Manyara National Park. Head to Manyara to see tree-climbing lions and flocks of flamingos.

Days 4–5: Central Serengeti. Drive into the Serengeti National Park. Spend two full days tracking predators and witnessing incredible biodiversity.

Day 6: Serengeti to Ngorongoro. Enjoy a morning game drive in the Serengeti, then head toward the Ngorongoro Highlands.

Day 7: Ngorongoro Crater. Descend 600 meters into the crater floor for a full-day game drive with a high chance of spotting the endangered black rhino.

Day 8: Fly to Zanzibar. Drive back to Arusha and take a short regional flight to Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) on the islands.The Beach & Culture (Days 9–15)

Days 9–11: Matemwe or Nungwi Beaches. Relax on the northern and eastern coasts at renowned spots like Nungwi Beach. Enjoy diving, snorkeling, or lounging.

Days 12–13: Paje or Jambiani. Move to the southeastern coast to experience a laid-back village vibe, popular for kitesurfing and pristine sandbars.


Day 14: Stone Town. Explore the Stone Town UNESCO World Heritage Site. Take a guided walking tour through its winding alleys, and enjoy a traditional Spice Tour.


Day 15: Departure. Transfer to the airport in Zanzibar for your international departure flight home.

DAY HIKING KILIMANJARO 

ITINERARY 


You will be picked up in the morning at Arusha or hotel in Moshi and drive taken to the Kilimanjaro National Park Gate (Marangu Gate, 1860m) drive time depending on the travel pick up location!the necessary gate registration has been completed, your trek can begin and you will soon be hiking through the dense Kilimanjaro rainforest.
 

Alongside the impressive vegetation, you will have the chance to catch a glimpse of black and white colobus monkeys, blue monkeys and various bird species. Our knowledgeable English-speaking guide will inform you about Kilimanjaro and will explain the different species of plants and animals you will see. You will also meet mountain climbers descending from the top, and perhaps some of them will tell you of their experiences and you can start to dream about a climb of your own!



After a 3-4 hour hike, you will reach Mandara Hut (2700m), where you will have a well-earned rest and lunch. Your guide will then take you on a short hike (15 minutes walking distance) to the Maundi Crater, where you can enjoy wonderful views of Mount Kilimanjaro, Northern Tanzania and Kenya. Once you have thoroughly enjoyed the views, you will return to the Marangu Gate (2-3 hours walking distance), where your vehicle will be waiting for your transfer back to your hotel. On your return journey you can enjoy the sunset and the day‘s memories.

CAMPING SAFARI BUDGET SERENGETI & CHEMKA


ITINERARY 


Day 1 - Arusha to Central Serengeti

Your safari will begin by 8AM in the morning with a pick up from your hotel at Arusha,will head towards Serengeti National Park the 240 Km drive will be covered in about 6hrs The Serengeti,means endless plains in the local African language and watching the grassland stretch out as far as the eye can see and eventually merging with the sky at the horizon is an absolutely breathtaking view. With an overall land area of 14,763 Sq Kms, It puts our notion about distance to shame. The Serengeti also houses the glorious “Big 5” (elephant, rhino, buffalo,lion and leopard). Along with the “Big 5”a very pleasant sighting would be one of Africa’s most beautiful animal – The Impala. The Serengeti ecosystem supports the greatest remaining concentration of plains game in Africa. The legendary Serengeti National Park is the most renowned safari destination for its incredible population of lions and leopards. The central portion of the Serengeti - known as Seronera area is one of the richest wildlife habitats in the park. It features the Seronera River, which provides a valuable water source to this area and therefore attracts wildlife well representative of most of the Serengeti’s species. In seronera, one must really look out for the many lion prides that thrive in this region. You must also look out for the Serengeti “Kopjes” which are massive boulders of granite standing in a sea of grass. They provide ample shelter to a large variety of flora and fauna picnic lunch will be done during the day long game drive itself the eventful day comes to an end with a heavy dinner and a good night’s rest in your accommodation Tents. 

Day 2 - Central Serengeti to Ngorongoro. 

After breakfast, you will have a morning game drive in Serengeti National Park Later depart for Ngorongoro Conservation Area with picnic lunch. Ngorongoro Crater is the largest collapsed volcanic crater in the world and fourteen kilometers of isolated natural beauty Ngorongoro Crater is surrounded by a ring of extinct volcanoes and the floor, dotted with watering holes,shelters some 30,000 animals th eventful day comes to an end with a heavy dinner and a good night’s rest in your accommodation Tents. 

Day 3 - Ngorongoro Crater Tour (5 hours) to Arusha or Kilimanjaro. 

On the last day of your safari, you will have an early start Finishing up with a quick breakfast you will make an early descent at around 6:30 AM into the crater floor the Ngorongoro Crater is the world’s largest inactive, intact and unfilled volcanic caldera. It has a massive floor of about 260 sq kms with a depth of over 2000 feet the 5-hour game drive on the crater floor will show you a lot of animal action. Keeping the camera ready is definitely recommended the African elephant,buffalo, Black rhino,Hippos, Hyenas,cheetahs and Lions are found in plenty Post the picnic lunch at the beautiful Hippo pool, you will begin a steep ascend to the top exit of the crater this is the last leg of your safari, with a 4hr drive left to Arusha You will be dropped off to your preferred location in Arusha 6:00 PM in the evening for overnight at lodge.
